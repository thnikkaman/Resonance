The Resonance implementation adapts the 16 by 8 song-title mesh, progress
curves, two-pass blending, and feedback-surface burn behavior from MilkDrop
2.25c's milkdropfs.cpp. Platform-specific OpenGL ES coordinates, texture
upload, and text rasterization replace the original Direct3D 9 and D3DX code.
